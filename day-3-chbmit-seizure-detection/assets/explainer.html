What the new model sees. Activity as a multiple of this patient's own baseline

15 min of the same recording. Dashed: the patient's normal level (1x). Amber: seizure.

4.3xaverage during the seizure

During this seizure, activity averages 4.3 times this patient's baseline (peak 7.5x). Rescaling every window to the same size erases that rise. In cross-validation, keeping it raised sensitivity from 45% to 54% and cut false alarms from 1.56 to 0.71 per hour.
